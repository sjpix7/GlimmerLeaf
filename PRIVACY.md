# Privacy Policy: Glimmerleaf

_Effective date: November 20, 2026_

Glimmerleaf ("the app") is published by sj.pixels ("we"). This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## Summary

**We do not collect, receive or share any personal data.** The app works entirely offline and has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking or third-party SDKs.

## What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

- your progress through the 28-day journey, completed practices, journal answers and mood
- your daily small wins and thanks
- your gratitude journal entries, with the date and time you wrote or edited them
- your settings (theme, language, reading voice and reminder times)

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We cannot see it.

## Device backup

If Android's backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your personal device backup (for example, your Google account backup), so you can restore it on a new phone. This backup is created and controlled by Android and your device settings, not by us, and we cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device settings.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** to remind you of your daily practice. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
- **Alarms and reminders (exact alarms)**, optional, so reminders arrive at the exact time. Without it, reminders arrive within about 10 minutes.
- **Run at startup** to restore your reminders after the phone restarts or the time zone changes.
- **Text-to-speech:** "Listen" uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. The text is passed to that engine on your device.

## Sharing

Nothing is shared unless you choose to. If you tap "Share" in History, the app hands your notes to the app you pick (for example email or messages), and that app's privacy policy applies.

## Deleting your data

You can delete individual journeys, days, sessions, daily entries and journal entries in the app, reset the journey in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data from your device.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
